# My Favorite Series: F.R.I.E.N.D.S.

---

## ☕ Quick Facts

F.R.I.E.N.D.S. is an iconic American sitcom that follows the lives of six young adults living in Manhattan.

> **Key Quote:** "Could I *be* any more excited?!" - Chandler Bing

## ✨ Why I Love It

* **Humor:** The show is incredibly funny, with **witty dialogue** and memorable running gags.
* **Characters:** Each character is *unique* and relatable, creating fantastic dynamics.
* **Nostalgia:** It's a comfort show that always brings a smile to my face.
* **Setting:** The Central Perk coffee shop is an **iconic** hangout spot.
